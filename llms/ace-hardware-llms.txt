# Ace Hardware

> Ace Hardware is a retailer-owned hardware cooperative with more than 5,000 locally owned and operated stores across all 50 U.S. states and 70+ countries. Stores stock paint, tools, lawn and garden equipment, plumbing and electrical supplies, grills, outdoor living, and seasonal goods, and are known for in-store services like paint matching, key cutting, propane exchange, and small-engine repair. Most stores are independently owned, so inventory, services, and hours vary by location — the Store Locator is the authoritative source for what a specific store carries and offers.

## Departments

Top-level category landing pages — Ace's full assortment is organized under these eleven departments.

- [Tools](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/tools ): Power tools, hand tools, accessories, storage, and workwear. Strongest in DEWALT, Milwaukee, CRAFTSMAN, DIABLO.
- [Lawn and Garden](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/lawn-and-garden ): Mowers, trimmers, gardening tools, watering, fertilizer, outdoor power equipment. Strongest in STIHL, EGO, Scotts, Roundup.
- [Paint and Supplies](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/paint-and-supplies ): Interior/exterior paint, primer, stain, painting tools. Brand partners: Benjamin Moore, Clark+Kensington, Magnolia Home, Valspar.
- [Hardware](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/hardware ): Fasteners, fire safety, locks, chain, and rope.
- [Plumbing](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/plumbing ): Fittings, fixtures, water heaters, drain care.
- [Lighting and Electrical](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/lighting-and-electrical ): Bulbs, fixtures, smart home, wiring; brand depth in Feit.
- [Heating and Cooling](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/heating-and-cooling ): Heaters, air conditioners, fans, thermostats.
- [Home and Decor](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/home-and-decor ): Cleaning, small appliances, kitchen, apparel, canning.
- [Outdoor Living](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/outdoor-living ): Grills and smokers (Weber, Traeger, Big Green Egg), patio furniture, coolers (YETI), pond supplies, sporting goods.
- [Building Supplies](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/building-supplies ): Lumber, drywall, ladders, insulation.
- [Automotive, RV and Marine](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/automotive-rv-and-marine ): Auto care, batteries, fluids, RV/marine accessories.
